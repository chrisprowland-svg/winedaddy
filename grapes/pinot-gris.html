Pinot Gris

Pinot Gris is a white grape variety that can produce crisp, refreshing wines or richer, more textural styles depending on ripeness and winemaking.

In one sentence: Pinot Gris is a white grape variety that can produce crisp, refreshing wines or richer, more textural styles depending on ripeness and winemaking.
ColourWhite wine
Typical fruitPear, apple, citrus
Style rangeCrisp to textural

Pinot Gris or Pinot Grigio?

They are the same grape. In practice, “Pinot Grigio” often signals a lighter, crisper style, while “Pinot Gris” often signals more texture and weight, although labels do not guarantee style.

What does it taste like?

Common descriptions include pear, apple, citrus and gentle spice. Texture can range from light and clean to broad and rounded.

Where does it grow in Australia?

Cool and moderate regions can retain freshness while building flavour. Mornington Peninsula is one established Australian region for the variety.

What food suits Pinot Gris?

Seafood, salads, roast chicken, light pasta, soft cheeses and dishes with gentle spice are natural matches.
