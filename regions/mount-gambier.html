Mount Gambier

Mount Gambier is an Australian Geographical Indication within South Australia’s Limestone Coast zone. Its viticultural landscape combines coastal influence, limestone and areas shaped by volcanic activity.

In one sentence: Mount Gambier is an Australian Geographical Indication within South Australia’s Limestone Coast zone. Its viticultural landscape combines coastal influence, limestone and areas shaped by volcanic activity.
StateSouth Australia
ZoneLimestone Coast
GI established2010

Where is Mount Gambier?

It sits in the south-east of South Australia within the Limestone Coast zone.

What shapes the region?

The area is underlain by sedimentary limestone, with volcanic cones, craters and scattered igneous geology adding to its distinct landscape.

What is the climate like?

Wine Australia describes a Mediterranean climate with warm to hot, dry summers and mild to cool winters, moderated by proximity to the sea.

Why does it matter for wine?

Cool conditions and varied geology can support fresh, elegant styles, especially from varieties such as Pinot Noir and Pinot Gris.
