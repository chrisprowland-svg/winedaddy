Mornington Peninsula

Mornington Peninsula is a strongly maritime Victorian wine region surrounded by Bass Strait, Port Phillip Bay and Western Port Bay.

In one sentence: Mornington Peninsula is a strongly maritime Victorian wine region surrounded by Bass Strait, Port Phillip Bay and Western Port Bay.
StateVictoria
ClimateStrongly maritime
Leading grapesPinot Noir, Chardonnay

What defines the region?

No vineyard site is more than seven kilometres from the ocean, giving the region a powerful maritime influence.

What are its major grapes?

Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Gris/Grigio are the leading varieties.

What is Mornington Pinot like?

Typical wines are light to medium bodied, with cherry and strawberry fruit, vibrant acidity and soft tannins.

What is Mornington Chardonnay like?

Cool conditions support pure-fruited, restrained wines, often with melon, white peach and citrus characters.
