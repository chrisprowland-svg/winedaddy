Regions

Yarra Valley

The Yarra Valley is a large and diverse Victorian wine region east of Melbourne, with varied elevations, aspects and microclimates.

In one sentence: The Yarra Valley is a large and diverse Victorian wine region east of Melbourne, with varied elevations, aspects and microclimates.
StateVictoria
Key grapesPinot Noir, Chardonnay
GI established1996

What is the Yarra Valley known for?

Its key varieties include Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Shiraz and Cabernet Sauvignon.

Why are the styles diverse?

Differences in elevation, aspect and local climate allow producers to make a broad range of expressions.

What is Yarra Pinot like?

Wine Australia describes typical styles as light to medium bodied, often showing cherry, strawberry and plum.

What is Yarra Chardonnay like?

Elevated sites can produce restrained styles, while the broader region is known for medium-bodied, textured Chardonnay often showing white peach.
